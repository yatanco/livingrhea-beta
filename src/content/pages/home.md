# section-is-a-scientist-healer-and-innovator-known

**Is a scientist, healer and innovator known for being on the pulse of what the world needs, often before it knows it.**

For the past 20 years, Dr. Rhea has worked as a holistic health coach and toxicologist helping women reclaim their health through nutrition science, detox protocols, and more recently, fertility support and postpartum recovery. She’s Science-backed & Ayurveda-informed.

She is the co-founder of **Aste Wellness**, a ritual-led skincare brand rooted in Ayurvedic wisdom. From smoothies to psychedelics, breathwork to blockchain, Rhea has long been a voice at the leading edge of healing, blending ancient practices with future-facing technologies to empower more conscious, connected living.

With a **PhD in Molecular Toxicology and Nutritional Biochemistry**, **Certification in Integrative Nutrition & Health Coaching**, and **Teacher Trainings in Kriya Yoga and Sound Ceremony**, her work bridges East and West, bringing science and soul into sacred conversation.

An **award-winning Mantra Artist**, Rhea released her debut EP, Soul Ceremony, in 2021, an immersive collection of sacred sounds designed to awaken presence, peace, and connection. Her music is an extension of her healing work, weaving ancient mantras into modern soundscapes to invite stillness and transformation.

Rhea is founding team member of **Diamond Therapeutics**, a mental health company pioneering non-hallucinogenic psychedelics, the founder of **Global Smoothie Day**, a movement to inspire healthy living, Co-founder of **Bowhead Health**, a privacy-first health tech company leveraging AI for clinical trials, and **Reset**, a tech-free wellness camp designed to reconnect adults to self.

Her work is a remembrance: that science can be sacred, and that healing begins within.

# section-i-was-born-in-a-small-northern

I was born in a small Northern Canadian town to Indian immigrant parents. My mother from India, and my father a refugee from East Africa. **My earliest memories are of navigating chronic health challenges with few resources, but anchored by a quiet resilience and unwavering hope.**

Years later, while completing my PhD in Molecular Toxicology, a personal health crisis forced me to turn the scientific lens inward. **That was the beginning of my healing – through food, ritual, and a reconnection to breath.**

When **I lost my father** to digestive cancer, that healing deepened.

When **I became a mother**, it was tested all over again.

These moments have shaped the way I see wellness – not as perfection, but as devotion.

**Today, I help others reclaim their health as a holistic health coach and toxicologist, through ancient wisdom, conscious science, and a remembering of what’s already within.**
