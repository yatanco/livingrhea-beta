# section-kriya-breathwork-and-sound-yoga

### Kriya Breathwork and Sound Yoga

# section-in-2019-i-traveled-to-rishikesh-india

In 2019, I traveled to Rishikesh, India — the birthplace of yoga — to study under Himalayan masters and reconnect with my ancestral roots. There, on the banks of the Ganges in the Himalayan foothills, I earned my **200-hour certification in ancient Vedic Kriya and Kundalini Breathwork**. In 2020, I deepened my training with an additional **100-hour Prenatal Kriya certification**.

**Kriya, meaning “action that leads to liberation,” is a powerful blend of breath, movement, and sound designed to clear energy channels, calm the nervous system, and connect us to higher consciousness.** Unlike modern yoga’s focus on movement alone, Kriya works on the subtle body — **detoxifying, awakening dormant energy, and aligning us with our dharmic path**.

These ancient techniques have transformed my own life, leaving me feeling energetically clear, deeply connected to my soul, and grounded in purpose. **Today, I share them to help others access the same inner stillness, vitality, and expansion.**
