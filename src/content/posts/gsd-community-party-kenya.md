GSD COMMUNITY PARTY KENYA!

Global Smoothie Day joined **Sandbox**’s annual gathering in Kilifi, on Kenya’s coast, at **Beneath the Baobabs**. Global innovators came together for storytelling, cultural excursions on traditional dhows, and a celebration of health and community, one smoothie at a time.
